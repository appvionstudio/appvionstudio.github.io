The honest reason is cost without the usual trade-off. A senior studio in Pakistan charges a fraction of a US or European agency, and you can judge the work on a small engagement before committing to a build.

Rates

AppVion charges a blended $45 to $65 an hour, against $150 to $250 at a US agency and $50 to $100 in Eastern Europe. A production app typically lands at $12,000 to $35,000. The full comparison is on the pricing page.

Pakistan Standard Time is UTC+5: a full working overlap with Europe, the Middle East and Asia, and a morning overlap with the eastern United States. Calls are scheduled inside your working hours.

Language

English for all project communication, documentation and code. The team also speaks Urdu.

Contracts

How pricing, payments and ownership work.

A fixed price per engagement

Once the scope is clear you get a fixed figure, and delivery runs against milestones. If the scope changes mid-build, the change is priced and agreed before it is built.

Milestone payments

A deposit to begin, and the balance released as each milestone is delivered and reviewed. You see working software at every stage.

You own everything

On final payment you own the source code, the repository, the design files, the store listings and the backend accounts. AppVion keeps no licence and holds no accounts.

No lock-in, NDA on request

Standard Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and mainstream backends, so another competent team can pick the project up. An NDA can be signed before any detailed discussion of your product.

FAQ

Common questions before the first call.

How much does mobile app development cost in Pakistan?

At AppVion Studio a Product Clarity Sprint starts at $1,500, and a production Android or Kotlin Multiplatform app costs $12,000 to $35,000. Senior engineering rates in Pakistan run $40 to $80 an hour at the top tier; AppVion charges a blended $45 to $65. The full breakdown is on the pricing page.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. The studio is remote-first and works in English with clients abroad through email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and scheduled calls, which are booked inside the client's working hours.

Do you build for iOS as well as Android?

Yes. Android Native is the deepest specialism here, and Kotlin Multiplatform is used when a product needs both platforms without duplicating business logic. Shared logic lives in one Kotlin module while each platform keeps a native interface.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

A first release with a focused feature set typically takes 8 to 12 weeks. Multi-role operational platforms with dispatch, approvals, live tracking or payments take 12 to 16 weeks and ship in focused releases, so progress stays inspectable throughout.

Why should I trust an offshore studio?

You should not, on assertion alone. That is why AppVion publishes working prototypes rather than testimonials, names every team member with a profile page, and lists direct contact routes to the founder. Start with a $1,500 scoping sprint and judge the work before committing to a build.

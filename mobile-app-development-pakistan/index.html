Why this matters

Most app projects fail on scope, not on code.

The common failure in this market is not that an app cannot be built. It is that the wrong app gets built — features nobody uses, a workflow that does not match how the business actually operates, and a launch that quietly stalls. Strategy is where the money is either saved or lost.

Founder-level accountability

Strategy stays close to execution. The person shaping the product direction is the person reviewing the implementation, so decisions move quickly and nothing gets lost in account-manager relay.

Built for real conditions

Interfaces are planned for thumbs, weak signal, permission prompts, empty states, and daily operational use — not for a demo on a fast connection.

Server-side where it counts

Anything involving money, access, or safety gets enforced on the server as well as the client. A client-side rule alone is a suggestion, not a guarantee.

Modern, durable architecture

Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform, Supabase, Ktor, and SQLDelight — chosen so the product can keep evolving after launch instead of needing a rewrite.

FAQ

Common questions before the first call.

How much does mobile app development cost in Pakistan?

Cost depends on scope rather than platform. A focused MVP with one user role and a straightforward backend sits at the low end; a multi-role operational platform with realtime dispatch, payments, and proof capture sits considerably higher. We scope and price after a strategy session rather than from a rate card, because an accurate number requires knowing the workflow, the user roles, and the integrations first.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. The studio is remote-first and works with international clients through email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and scheduled strategy calls. Projects have covered logistics, field service dispatch, and SaaS automation across different markets.

Do you build for iOS as well as Android?

Yes. Android Native is the deepest specialism here, and Kotlin Multiplatform is used when a product needs both platforms without duplicating business logic. Shared logic lives in one Kotlin module while each platform keeps a native interface.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

A focused MVP typically runs a few months from strategy to launch-ready build. Larger operational platforms with multiple roles, realtime features, and payment handling take longer and ship in focused releases rather than one large drop, so progress stays inspectable throughout.

What should I prepare before the first call?

A short brief covering the product idea, who uses it, which platforms you need, what stage you are at, a budget range, and your preferred launch timeline. That is enough to have a genuinely useful first conversation rather than a generic one.

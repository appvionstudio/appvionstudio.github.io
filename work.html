Field service platform · Kotlin Multiplatform

ProFix

ProFix closes the loop between a reported fault and a signed-off repair. Jobs are ranked by SLA risk and matched to a qualified contractor, work is evidenced with before and after photographs on site, and a manager approves remotely with the full record in front of them. The business rules live in shared Kotlin so they cannot drift between platforms.

  • DispatchEvery open job ranked by SLA risk, with the right contractor already matched.
  • Job detailOne screen holds the scope, the site history, and the contractor already on the way.
  • Proof of workBefore and after evidence is captured on site, so approvals stop depending on phone calls.
  • ApprovalManagers approve remotely with the full record in front of them - no site visit required.
  • OperationsThe numbers owners actually ask for: throughput, first-time fix rate, and where time is lost.

Enterprise operations · Android Native

SnowGrid

SnowGrid runs a snow-removal business as a live network rather than a phone tree. Property owners request service and trace their own lot on a map to get a price; the operations centre sees crews and jobs on a live dispatch map; drivers get tasks, capture photo evidence, and track payouts. Pricing is computed from snow depth, surface area, and priority rather than negotiated per call.

  • Fleet intelligenceThe operator opens to daily revenue, active operations, and escrow balance - the three numbers that decide the day.
  • Live dispatchCrews and properties on one live map, so assignment is a decision rather than a phone call.
  • Fleet operationsEvery job carries its own approval state and payout, pending through approved, with nothing tracked off-system.
  • Pricing engineSnow depth, surface area, and priority resolve into a quoted price the customer can authorise on the spot.
  • Customer requestThe property owner side stays deliberately small: pick the site, see the price, authorise the work.
  • Site measurementOwners trace their own lot on the map and the app returns area and perimeter that the pricing engine then uses.
  • Driver portalDrivers get tasks, photo evidence, and payout history in a portal built for gloves and bad weather.

Outreach infrastructure · Android Native

GMassPulse

GMassPulse puts cold-email operations on a phone. Leads import straight from a Google Sheet and are validated before anything is queued, campaigns are composed with merge fields, follow-up steps are scheduled in the recipient's timezone, and a throttling engine enforces daily sending limits so a campaign cannot quietly burn the sending domain.

  • Pulse dashboardLifetime sends and opens first, campaign list underneath - the operator sees performance before controls.
  • Import leadsLeads pull straight from a Google Sheet and get validated before a single email is queued.
  • Campaign editorSubject, body, and merge fields composed on a phone without the layout fighting the keyboard.
  • Auto follow-upsFollow-up steps are scheduled with timezone-aware sending, because reply rate lives in the sequence.
  • Deliverability engineDaily limits and throttling are enforced in the product, so a campaign cannot quietly burn the domain.

University product · Android Native

WUM Connect

WUM Connect gives a university one communication channel with four audiences. Students, faculty, staff, and administrators open the same app and see only what applies to their role, with read receipts so the registrar knows a notice actually landed. Campus wifi is unreliable, so the app caches aggressively and queues writes until it reconnects.

  • Campus feedStudents, faculty, and admin each open the same app and see only what applies to them.
  • AnnouncementDelivery is measurable - the admin sees exactly who has read a notice and who has not.
  • RolesAccess is structural, not cosmetic - permissions are enforced at the data layer.
  • Offline syncCampus wifi drops constantly. The app queues writes locally and reconciles on reconnect.
  • ProfileIdentity, enrolment, and notification control in one predictable place.

Data-heavy utility · Android Native

Attendance Suite

Attendance Suite handles institutional attendance at a scale that breaks naive implementations. Paging 3 keeps a four-thousand-row roster scrolling smoothly on mid-range hardware, exceptions such as late marks and duplicate scans are grouped so a coordinator clears them in one pass, and reporting happens in the app rather than in a spreadsheet afterwards.

  • SessionsToday's sessions with live capture status, so nothing is marked twice or missed.
  • Live rosterPaging 3 keeps a 4,000-row roster scrolling at 60fps on mid-range hardware.
  • Student recordOne student, one history - the view a department head opens before a decision.
  • ReportsInstitutional reporting without a spreadsheet export step in the middle.

Sports Society

Sports Society Events coordinates campus sport without a waitlist spreadsheet. Filters stack rather than replacing one another, entries lock automatically when a slot limit is reached, and a confirmed entry lives in the app, works offline, and is scannable at the gate.

  • EventsWhat is open, what is closing, and what already filled - readable in one glance.
  • FiltersFilters combine instead of replacing each other - the detail that decides whether people use it.
  • Event detailEverything a participant needs before committing, without a second screen.
  • RegistrationThree fields, one confirmation. Drop-off happens where forms get greedy.
  • My entryThe confirmation lives in the app, works offline, and is scannable at the gate.

Logistics · Kotlin Multiplatform

D. Delivery

A delivery and logistics platform built as three pieces: a customer app for booking, live tracking and payment; a rider app for accepting jobs, navigating and recording proof; and a web control tower for dispatch, the ledger and disputes. Every parcel is recorded on video at pickup and again at drop-off, and those two recordings are the evidence behind a parcel insurance product.

  • Booking and live trackingCustomers book a delivery and watch it move in real time.
  • Dual video proofPickup and drop-off recordings are mandatory and stored server side.
  • Expanding radius dispatchOffered to the nearest riders first, widening if nobody accepts.
  • Rider walletCash on delivery reconciled through a settlement ledger.
  • Dynamic pricingRouted distance, weight surcharge and a fragile-handling fee.
  • Control towerWeb admin for dispatch, disputes and the ledger.

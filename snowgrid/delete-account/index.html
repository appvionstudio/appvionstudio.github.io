Delete your SnowGrid account

SnowGrid mobile app (com.snowgrid.app) · AppVion Studio · Updated 14 August 2026

You can delete your SnowGrid account and personal data at any time. You do not need the app installed to make the request.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SnowGrid and sign in.
  2. Tap the account button in the top right of your dashboard.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

This takes effect straight away. Your sign-in stops working immediately and cannot be restored.

Option 2 — Request by email (no app needed)

Fill this in and it will open your email app with the request ready to send. Nothing is submitted from this page and no information is stored here.

Not working? Email privacy@appvionstudio.com with the subject “SnowGrid account deletion request” and include the email address on your account.

What happens next

  • We confirm you own the account by replying to the email address on it. We will not act on a request we cannot verify — otherwise anyone could delete someone else's account.
  • We complete the deletion and confirm it to you, normally within 7 days and always within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Being straight about this matters more than sounding reassuring.

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in and passwordPermanently deleted
Your name, email address, phone numberErased
Your service address and any notes about youErased
Stored location (contractor accounts)Erased
Push notification tokenDeleted
Jobs you booked or worked onKept by the company, no longer linked to your name
Payment records for those jobsKept as the company's financial records

Job and payment records stay with the snow removal company you dealt with because they are that company's own financial and tax records — evidence of work performed and money owed or paid. They are not ours to erase. After deletion they are no longer connected to a name, email address or phone number.

If you run a snow removal company

Company admin accounts are deleted by us, not in the app

Deleting a company account also removes its contractors, its live jobs, and payment records other people depend on — including money still owed to the crew. That is not something to trigger with a single tap, so we handle it with you directly.

Email support@appvionstudio.com and we will confirm what will be removed before anything is deleted.

How it works

How staff mark attendance without a biometric machine.

The phone each employee already carries does the job a fingerprint machine used to do.

01

We set it up

We configure the system for your institution's Wi-Fi network and your staff. Full setup takes about 4 to 5 days.

02

Staff connect to your Wi-Fi

Each employee connects their own mobile phone to the institution's Wi-Fi network.

03

They mark attendance on the phone

Connected to the Wi-Fi, the employee marks attendance in the app. No fingerprint scanner is involved.

04

No hardware to look after

No biometric machines to buy or license, and no machine hardware problems to chase.

Proof

Do we need fingerprint or biometric machines?

No. Staff mark attendance on their own mobile phones while connected to your institution's Wi-Fi. There is no fingerprint hardware to buy and no biometric machine licence to renew.

How long does setup take?

Full setup takes about 4 to 5 days. We configure the system for your institution's Wi-Fi network and your staff.

Where is this attendance system already in use?

At The Women University Multan, where more than 300 employees connect to the university Wi-Fi and mark attendance on their own phones instead of on biometric machines.

Do you build it from scratch for every institution?

No. AppVion Studio owns the complete code of the system and sets the same system up for each new institution. It is a private deployment for your institution, not a public app on Google Play.

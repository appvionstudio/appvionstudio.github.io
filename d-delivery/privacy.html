What the D. apps collect, and what we do with it.

Applies to the D. Customer app and the D. Rider app.

This policy explains what the D. apps collect, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It covers two separate apps published by AppVion Studio:

  • D. Customer (com.appvionstudio.ddelivery.customer) — used to book and track deliveries.
  • D. Rider (com.appvionstudio.ddelivery.rider) — used by our delivery riders to accept and complete jobs.

The two apps collect different information. Where that matters, the sections below say which app is being described. AppVion Studio, based in Lahore, Pakistan, is responsible for the data described here.

The short version. We collect what is needed to move a parcel from one person to another, and to pay the rider who moved it. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. Riders are located continuously while they are on duty — and only while they are on duty. You can delete your account from inside the app or by emailing us.

2. Location, explained plainly

This is the most sensitive thing the apps do, so it gets its own section rather than a line in a table.

  • Customers. Location is read only while you are actively picking a point on the map. Nothing is collected when the app is closed or in the background.
  • Riders. When you set yourself Online, the app starts a foreground service that reports your position to our servers roughly every ten seconds, and keeps doing so while the app is in the background. A permanent notification is shown the entire time this is happening — if that notification is not visible, location is not being collected.
  • Riders can stop it at any time by going Offline. Collection stops immediately.
  • Your live position is visible to the customer only for a delivery you are actively assigned to, and to our operations staff.
  • We do not build a movement history or profile from rider location. Only your most recent position is stored, and each update overwrites the last.

Android asks for your permission before any of this happens, and the app explains what it is about to do before that prompt appears. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings, though the Rider app cannot offer you jobs without it.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account.
  • To price, dispatch, route, track and complete deliveries.
  • To let the customer and rider contact each other about a live delivery.
  • To calculate rider earnings and reconcile cash-on-delivery amounts.
  • To investigate loss, damage, theft and disputes, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send operational notifications about your own deliveries.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix defects.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the apps and our servers uses HTTPS. The apps refuse unencrypted connections outright.
  • Your login session is stored on your device in Android's hardware-backed encrypted storage.
  • The Rider app's on-device database — which can hold customer names, addresses and item details while offline — is fully encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Access to records in our database is enforced at the database level, row by row. A customer's account can read only that customer's own orders; a rider can read only jobs they are assigned to or eligible for.
  • Rider identity documents (CNIC, driving licence, vehicle papers) are held in a private storage area that is not publicly readable. They are accessible only to the rider who uploaded them and to authorised AppVion Studio staff.
  • Photographs uploaded through the apps are re-encoded before upload, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS coordinates from the original camera file.

No system is perfectly secure and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities without undue delay.

We are direct about the exception because it matters: we cannot delete completed delivery and payment records. They evidence money that changed hands, cash a rider collected on our behalf, and goods we were responsible for. When you delete your account we strip your name, phone number, photo and saved addresses from those records so they can no longer be connected to you, but the transaction itself remains in our books.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information. Your profile is editable inside the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete your account. See section 8.
  • Turn off location. Android Settings → Apps → D. → Permissions. Riders can also simply go Offline.
  • Turn off notifications. Android Settings, or the app's own notification settings.
  • Complain. Email us first — we would rather fix it. You may also raise the matter with the relevant Pakistani authority.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in two ways:

  • In the app: open Profile and choose Delete my account.
  • Without the app: use the account deletion page.

Deletion is refused while you still have a delivery in progress, and for riders, while you are still holding cash on delivery that has not been settled with the office. Finish or settle those first. Full detail of what is erased and what is retained is on the deletion page.

9. Children

The D. apps are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and move the “last updated” date. Where a change materially affects you, we will also notify you in the app.

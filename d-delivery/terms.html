1. What D. is

D. is a courier and delivery service. Customers book a delivery through the D. Customer app; our riders collect the item and deliver it to the address given, using the D. Rider app. We currently operate in and around Lahore, within the service area shown in the app. A booking outside that area will be refused at the time of booking.

  • You must provide accurate information, including a working phone number.
  • You are responsible for everything done through your account. Do not share your login.

3. Accounts

  • Customers sign in with a Google account.
  • Riders do not self-register. Rider accounts are created by AppVion Studio, and a rider must submit CNIC, driving licence and vehicle documents and be approved before receiving any job. We may reject or revoke approval at our discretion, including where documents are unclear, expired or inconsistent.

4. Bookings, fares and payment

  • The fare is calculated and shown in the app before you confirm a booking. It is based on distance and, where applicable, the declared value of the item. The fare shown at confirmation is the fare that applies.
  • Fares are calculated by our servers. Any figure produced on your device is an estimate for display only.
  • Payment methods are Cash on Delivery, Easypaisa and JazzCash, as offered in the app. We do not collect or store card numbers or wallet credentials.
  • For Cash on Delivery, the amount is collected in cash at the point stated in your booking. Please have the correct amount available; riders may not carry change.
  • We may refuse or cancel a booking — for example where the address cannot be reached, the item is prohibited, or we reasonably suspect fraud.

5. What you may not send

You must not book a delivery for any of the following, and you confirm at the time of booking that your item is not among them:

  • Illegal drugs, narcotics or controlled substances.
  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives, fireworks or flammable liquids and gases.
  • Live animals.
  • Human remains or body parts.
  • Currency, bullion, bearer instruments, or negotiable securities.
  • Stolen goods, counterfeit goods, or anything you are not legally entitled to send.
  • Hazardous, toxic, corrosive, radioactive or biologically infectious material.
  • Perishable goods requiring temperature control.
  • Anything whose carriage is prohibited by Pakistani law.

If a rider discovers a prohibited item, the delivery will be stopped, the fare is not refunded, and we may report the matter to the authorities. Repeated or serious breaches will result in your account being closed.

6. Packaging and declared value

  • You are responsible for packaging your item well enough to survive normal handling on a motorcycle in city traffic. Fragile items must be protected accordingly.
  • Where the app asks you to declare a value, you must declare it honestly. Insurance cover and any claim are based on the declared value.
  • An under-declared value limits what you can claim. An over-declared value does not increase what we will pay — claims are settled on the genuine value of the item, evidenced by receipt or invoice.
  • For phone shipments you must provide the correct IMEI, brand, model and condition. A claim on a device whose IMEI does not match the booking will be refused.

7. Delivery, proof and collection

  • Delivery times shown in the app are estimates. Traffic, weather and access at either end affect them, and we do not guarantee a specific arrival time.
  • Riders record a short video at pickup and at drop-off, and take the recipient's signature. This is our proof of what was handed over and in what condition, and it protects both sides in a dispute.
  • Someone must be available at the drop-off address to receive the item and, for Cash on Delivery, to pay. If nobody is available, the rider will attempt to contact the number given. A failed delivery may still be charged.
  • You confirm you are entitled to give us the recipient's name and phone number for the purpose of the delivery.

8. Cancellation

  • A customer may cancel a booking free of charge while it is still pending, before a rider has accepted it. The Cancel option in the app reflects this.
  • Once a rider has accepted, the booking can no longer be cancelled in the app. Contact support — a charge may apply, particularly if the rider has already travelled to the pickup point.
  • We may cancel a booking at any time where it is unsafe, unlawful, or impossible to complete. Where we cancel through no fault of yours, you are not charged.

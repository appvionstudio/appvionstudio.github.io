Where the saving comes from

Adding iOS adds 35 to 55 percent, not 100.

Kotlin Multiplatform shares the expensive part of an app, the rules, once. On a $12,000 Android-only scope, adding iOS this way typically adds about $4,200 to $6,600, not another $12,000.

Written once, in Kotlin

Domain models and business rules, validation, networking and API contracts, the local database and the offline sync logic.

Built for each platform

The interface, navigation, permissions and system dialogs, camera and location, push notifications and secure credential storage.

The optional extra saving

Compose Multiplatform can share the screens too, which cuts interface work further. The trade is an interface that follows each platform's conventions a little less closely.

What moves the number

Seven things decide almost every quote.

Screen count is the weakest predictor of cost. These are the strongest.

Number of user roles

One role is a list and a detail screen. Four roles with different permissions is four products sharing a backend. This is the biggest driver on operational apps.

Whether the data is live

Pull-to-refresh is cheap. Screens that update the moment a driver moves need realtime infrastructure, conflict handling and much more testing.

Offline behaviour

Working offline means a local database, a sync queue and a decision about conflicting edits. In Kotlin Multiplatform that logic is written once for both platforms. How offline-first works.

Integrations

Payments, maps, SMS, calendars and existing ERP or CRM systems each bring their own edge cases. Three integrations often cost more than ten screens.

Interface ambition

Motion, empty states, loading and error states: the hundred small decisions that make an app feel expensive.

Platform count

Android only is the baseline. iOS through Kotlin Multiplatform adds 35 to 55 percent.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

How much does a Kotlin Multiplatform app cost?

At AppVion Studio a production Kotlin Multiplatform app costs $12,000 to $35,000. A focused first release lands at $12,000 to $18,000 over 8 to 12 weeks; a multi-role operational platform with dispatch, approvals, live tracking or payments runs $20,000 to $35,000 over 12 to 16 weeks.

Is Kotlin Multiplatform cheaper than building two native apps?

Usually, yes. Adding iOS through Kotlin Multiplatform typically adds 35 to 55 percent to an Android-only build rather than doubling it, because the business logic, networking and local database are written once and only the interface is built per platform.

Is Kotlin Multiplatform cheaper than Flutter?

For an interface-heavy app with little logic, Flutter is usually cheaper to start. For an operational app where a wrong rule costs money, Kotlin Multiplatform tends to cost less over the life of the product, because rules are written and fixed once. See Kotlin Multiplatform vs Flutter.

What is not included in the price?

Third-party costs that belong to you and are paid directly: the Apple Developer Program at $99 a year, Google Play Console at a one-off $25, and hosting, database, mapping, SMS or payment fees. Maintenance after launch is a separate retainer from $900 a month.

Why start with a Clarity Sprint?

Because the most expensive projects are the ones that change direction halfway through. The sprint, from $1,500 over one to two weeks, fixes the scope, the workflow map and the technical direction, including whether Kotlin Multiplatform is the right call at all, and ends in a fixed quote.

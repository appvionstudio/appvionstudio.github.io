Project Manager | AppVion Studio Saif Malik Multan, Pakistan · working remotely with clients worldwide.

What he owns

Project delivery and the communication around it: scope kept honest, timelines kept real, and clients kept informed without having to chase.

Experience

Ten years working as a project manager across online platforms, delivering for the companies he has worked with.

Why it matters here

A founder-led studio is fast but easy to lose track of. A dedicated project manager is how a small team stays reliable to a client in another time zone.

In the studio

The person who makes delivery predictable.

Keeps projects moving

Tracks what is in progress, what is blocked, and what needs a decision — so engineering time is spent building rather than waiting.

One point of contact

Clients get a clear line into the studio and a straight answer on where their project stands, including when the answer is that something has slipped.

Maintains, not just launches

Work does not end at release. Saif carries projects through iteration and maintenance, where most agencies quietly stop paying attention.

Want to talk about a project?

Bring the product, the constraints and the deadline. You will get a straight read on what is realistic before anyone quotes you a number.

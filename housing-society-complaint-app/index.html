PropFix for housing societies

PropFix: a maintenance complaint app for housing societies and property offices in Pakistan.

PropFix is a maintenance complaint app on Google Play that a housing society or property office can download and run itself. Residents raise complaints, the society office assigns each job to a contractor, the contractor attaches before and after photos as proof, and the office approves the work. It is built by AppVion Studio, and you can start on the free plan.

How the complaint flow works

From a resident's complaint to an approved job.

Three PropFix roles map onto a society. The resident uses the tenant role, the society office uses the property manager role, and maintenance staff or outside contractors use the contractor role.

01

The resident raises a complaint

The resident links their unit with an invite code from the office, then reports the problem in the app. Each complaint shows its unit, category and urgency.

02

The office assigns it

The society office sees the waiting complaint on its PropFix board and assigns the job to a contractor from its own list.

03

The contractor adds photo proof

The contractor is notified of the job, does the work and attaches before and after photos. PropFix records where each photo was taken, at the moment it is taken.

04

The office approves

The office checks the photos on the job record and approves the work without a site visit. The resident follows the complaint's status in the app.

Why not WhatsApp and registers

Why PropFix beats phone calls, WhatsApp groups and complaint registers.

When complaints live in phone calls and messages, nothing is searchable, nobody can say which jobs are open without asking around, and "it's done" is a claim, not a record.

Complaints stay on record

A complaint made on a call or in a group chat is hard to find a week later. In PropFix every complaint and job is a record on the office's list.

Open jobs at a glance

The office board shows how many jobs are open, dispatched, in progress and completed, without calling anyone.

Proof, not a claim

Before and after photos sit on the job itself, so "it's done" can be checked.

Residents see the status

Residents follow their complaint's status in the app instead of calling the office for updates.

FAQ

PropFix for housing societies: questions.

Can a housing society use PropFix on its own?

Yes. Download PropFix from Google Play, sign up as the property manager for your society office, and invite residents and contractors with invite codes. If you want help setting it up, book a free 15-minute call.

How do residents and contractors join?

Both need an invite code from the office. Contractors sign up with a code the office creates under Settings, then Manage Invites. Residents enter the office's invite code on their dashboard to link their unit.

How does the office know the work was really done?

The contractor attaches before and after photos to the job, and PropFix stores the location at the moment each photo is taken. The office approves the job with those photos on the record, without a site visit.

Does PropFix work on a weak signal?

Yes. PropFix saves changes to the phone first and syncs when the phone is back online. Until then a change shows as pending.

How much does PropFix cost?

The Free plan covers up to 3 properties, 20 work orders a month and 3 contractors. Pro is $19 a month and Business is $49 a month, as listed in the PropFix terms of service. You upgrade from Settings in the app.

Information We Collect

When you submit a project brief, we may collect your name, email, project type, timeline, message, browser information, the page you sent it from, the page you first arrived on, and the website or search engine that referred you (for example Google or LinkedIn). We use this only to understand which pages bring enquiries.

How We Use It

We use this information to respond to inquiries, assess project fit, schedule calls, improve the website, and manage AppVion Studio's project pipeline.

Tools We Use

The website may use Firebase, FormSubmit, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Calendly, email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Instagram for contact, analytics, scheduling, and communication.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by trusted services that help us receive forms, measure website performance, schedule calls, and communicate with visitors.

Retention

Project inquiries may be kept while we evaluate, discuss, deliver, or support a project. You can request deletion of your inquiry data by emailing us.

Cookies and Analytics

The site asks before setting analytics cookies. If you allow them, Google Analytics measures visits and Microsoft Clarity records how pages are used (clicks and scrolling, with form fields masked) so we can improve the site. If you decline, Clarity does not load and Google Analytics only receives cookieless, aggregated signals. Contact buttons are counted by type and page, never with your email address or phone number.

Your Choices

You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted through the website.

Good practice

Keep the account safe

  • Use a long, unique password and never share it in chats or email.
  • If a password was ever shared, send yourself a reset link and change it.
  • Only the two owner emails in the Firestore rules can read leads or publish.
  • Lock the panel on shared computers.

Common questions

  • I'm a contractor and signup asks for an invite code. Ask the property manager who hired you. They create codes in the app under Settings → Manage Invites.
  • I'm a tenant and can't submit a request. Link your unit first: enter the invite code from your property manager on your dashboard.
  • My changes show "pending". PropFix saves to your phone first and syncs when you are back online. Open the app with a connection and it will catch up.
  • I forgot my password. Email us from the address you sign in with and we will help you reset it.

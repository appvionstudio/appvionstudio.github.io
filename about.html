About

A small studio that does the work itself.

AppVion Studio builds mobile products for businesses that need software to hold up under daily operational pressure. It is three people in Multan, Pakistan, working with clients worldwide.

In one paragraph

AppVion Studio is a founder-led mobile app development studio based in Multan, Punjab, Pakistan. It builds native Android applications in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and cross-platform products using Kotlin Multiplatform, with a focus on operational business systems: dispatch, approvals, attendance, field service, and internal tools. The studio works remotely with clients worldwide, in English, and responds to new enquiries within 24 hours. Engagements run from a $1,500 scoping sprint to a $35,000 full product build.

Why the studio is small on purpose.

Most agencies grow by inserting people between the client and the engineer. It is a reasonable way to scale revenue and a terrible way to keep a product coherent. Decisions get relayed, context is lost in the handoff, and the person who understood why a rule existed has moved to another account by month three.

AppVion is built the other way round. The founder does the strategy and writes the Android code, so the person deciding what to build is the person who knows what it costs to build it. That caps how many projects run at once. It also means nothing is lost in translation.

Proof you can inspect, not testimonials.

The site publishes no star ratings and no client quotes, because a studio at this size cannot produce a credible volume of them and invented ones are worse than none. Instead the work is made inspectable: six products described screen by screen, interactive prototypes you can click through on the home page, public video demos, and a downloadable APK.

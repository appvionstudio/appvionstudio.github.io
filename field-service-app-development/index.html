FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does the app work without signal?

Yes. We build offline-first: work is recorded on the phone and synced when the connection returns, and the app shows its own sync status so a field worker can tell the difference.

Can contractors use their own phones?

Yes. In PropFix contractors join by an invite code from the manager, so the contractor list stays curated rather than open to anyone.

Android, iOS or both?

Android native when your crews are on Android; Kotlin Multiplatform when you need both, so dispatch and pricing rules are written once.

What does a field service app cost?

Multi-role operational platforms run $20,000 to $35,000 over 12 to 16 weeks. A focused first release is $12,000 to $18,000. Most projects start with a $1,500 Clarity Sprint that ends in a fixed quote.

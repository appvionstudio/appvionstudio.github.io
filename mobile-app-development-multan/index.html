Why this matters

Most app projects fail on scope, not on code.

The common failure is not that an app cannot be built. It is that the wrong app gets built — features nobody uses, a workflow that does not match how the business actually operates, and a launch that quietly stalls. Strategy is where the money is either saved or lost.

Founder-level accountability

The person shaping the product direction is the person reviewing the implementation. Decisions move quickly and nothing gets lost in account-manager relay.

Built for real conditions

Interfaces planned for thumbs, weak signal, permission prompts, empty states, and daily operational use — not for a demo on a fast connection.

Server-side where it counts

Anything involving money, access, or safety is enforced on the server as well as the client. A client-side rule alone is a suggestion, not a guarantee.

Modern, durable architecture

Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform, Supabase, Ktor, and SQLDelight — chosen so the product can keep evolving after launch instead of needing a rewrite.

FAQ

Questions from Multan clients.

Where is AppVion Studio located in Multan?

We are on LMQ Road at Katchery Chowk, Multan, Punjab. Most project work runs remotely through scheduled calls, WhatsApp, and email, and in-person meetings in Multan can be arranged for local clients.

Do you only work with clients in Multan?

No. The studio is based in Multan but works remote-first with clients across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, the rest of Pakistan, and internationally. Delivered projects include a logistics platform, a fleet dispatch system, and an email automation product across different markets.

How much does an app cost?

Cost tracks scope, not location. A focused MVP with one user role sits at the low end; a multi-role operational platform with realtime dispatch, payments, and proof capture sits considerably higher. We price after a strategy session rather than from a rate card, because an accurate number needs the workflow, roles, and integrations defined first.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. For clients in and around Multan an in-person strategy meeting can be arranged. Book a call first so the session is scoped and genuinely useful rather than exploratory.

What should I prepare before the first call?

A short brief covering the product idea, who uses it, which platforms you need, what stage you are at, a budget range, and your preferred launch timeline.
